I am a **2025 cohort undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Zhejiang University**. This site is a small room I keep for myself: learning notes, project traces, recent thoughts, and Biying, who gives visitors a conversational way into the public parts of it.

I do not want it to read like a stiff profile. I would rather let the process show: what I am learning, why something caught my attention, and how a loose idea slowly becomes something real.

## How I Work

I like making an idea tangible first, then improving it through actual use. To me, code is not only for assignments or tests. It can also be a form of expression: turning vague interests into pages, tools, agents, and interactive systems that other people can genuinely open, experience, and answer back.

I also want the process to stay honest. Notes should keep more than conclusions; they should keep the route toward understanding. Project pages should show more than stacks; they should explain why I made certain choices, what still feels unfinished, and what I want to improve next.
