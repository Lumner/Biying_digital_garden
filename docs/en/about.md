I am a **Class of 2025 undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Zhejiang University**. This site is my public digital garden: a place for learning notes, project process, current status, and Biying, a digital persona that helps visitors enter the public content through conversation.

I do not want this place to feel like a stiff profile. I want it to feel more like a room that is still growing: you can see what I am learning and building, and also catch a little of my judgment, curiosity, and everyday texture.

## How I Work

I like making an idea tangible first, then improving it through actual use. To me, code is not only something for assignments or tests. It can also be a form of expression: turning vague interests into pages, tools, agents, and interactive systems that other people can open, experience, and respond to.

I also want the process to be honest. Notes should not only keep conclusions, but also the path toward understanding. Project pages should not only list stacks, but also explain why I made certain decisions, what constraints I met, and what I want to improve next.
