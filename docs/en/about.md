# About Me

I am a **Class of 2025 undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at XX University**. This site is my public digital garden: part learning record, part project archive, part living profile, and part conversational entrance through Biying.

I do not want this place to feel like a compressed profile. I want it to show a person in motion: someone with technical ambition, a sense of everyday life, a habit of building things, and the patience to write down how ideas become real.

## How I Work

I like making an idea tangible first, then improving it through actual use. To me, code is not only something for assignments or tests. It can also be a form of expression: turning vague interests into pages, tools, agents, interactive systems, and eventually things other people can experience.

I am also practicing writing down the process. Notes record how I understand concepts. Project pages record why I made certain decisions, what constraints I met, and what I want to improve next. Friends can see what I am exploring, and other visitors can read public evidence of my technical interests, learning speed, and communication style.
