This site will keep some less template-like material too: temporary thoughts, debugging notes, good things I read, and records with a bit of everyday warmth.

## Boundaries

Biying only reads content that is publicly published on this site. He does not touch local files, drafts, or private records. The site is not meant to be finished all at once; it will grow with my learning, projects, and writing.
