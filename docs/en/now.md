## Life Texture

Recently I have been trying to put learning, building, and daily life into the same rhythm: strengthening my AI foundations while making websites and tools that can actually be opened and used. This site will keep some less template-like material too: temporary thoughts, debugging notes, good things I read, and records with a little everyday warmth.

## Boundaries

Biying only reads content that has already been published on this site. He does not touch local files, drafts, or private records. The site is not meant to be finished all at once; it will grow with my learning, projects, and writing.
