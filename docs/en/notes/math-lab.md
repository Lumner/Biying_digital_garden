# Math Lab

This page tests math rendering inside `.md` files.

Inline example: $E = mc^2$, and $\alpha + \beta = \gamma$.

Block example:

$$
\nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}
$$

A matrix:

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
,\quad
\det(A) = -2
$$

If you see rendered formulas instead of raw LaTeX text, `mathjax.js` is working.
